CAPRI+ // HOW HER BRAIN THINKS

One thing you say, start to finish

Example: you say "tell Sam I'm running late, and remember his birthday is the 30th" while a game is open in the background.
1HearParakeet turns your voice into text on the PC. Her name is a hotword, and a misheard "Capree" still counts.
➜
2Quick route?Obvious commands (volume, stop, a setting) run from code with no model. This one isn't, so it goes on.
➜
3UnderstandWho is "Sam"? The name matcher finds People/Sam.md (sound-alikes and Lithuanian name forms too). Two Sams? She asks one short question.
➜
4The momentOne picture: time, where you are, the game, the window in front, her open job. Each path reads only the slice it needs.
➜
5ThinkHaiku gets one fresh request: her persona, Core notes, what Recall found, lessons, the last 32 messages.
6Pick a toolThe tool step chooses the discord tool with "running late" for Sam. Hands that click stay locked while the game runs.
➜
7CheckSay equals do: "sent it" is only said when the tool really sent. Guarded settings need your own clear words.
➜
8AnswerHer voice (or the bubble, or your phone, or Discord when you're away) and the action itself.
➜
9RememberNext to the reply, the memory pass writes "Birthday: 30 October" into People/Sam.md, with the day you said it.
➜
10KeepThe chat goes to the day's archive; "messaged Sam" goes to her action log, so later she knows it's done.
Her memory layers
LayerWhat it holdsWhen she reads it
Corewho you are, close people, routine, current projects; small on purposeevery message
Recent talklast 32 messages, rolling summary, the current goalevery message
NotesPeople, places, facts, each with a date and a link to the daywhen Recall finds them (word and meaning search)
Day pageswhat you did each day, from your own words"what did I do on Tuesday"
Chat archiveevery conversation, by dayquestions about the past
Plans + lessonspromises with dates; what she got wrongcheck-ins raise plans; lessons before she acts
Action logwhat she did, as short gistsso she never claims or repeats things
Why her context never piles up
Fresh every time. The model keeps nothing between messages. Every request is rebuilt from the parts above, and each part has a cap, so day 300 is as big as day 1.
Search, not stuffing. Older talk isn't sent along. It's found again when it matters: word search plus a local meaning model (bge-base, on your PC).
Changes keep history. "I don't go to the gym anymore" replaces the fact and moves the old one under History with its date. The night review catches missed saves and contradictions.

CAPRI+ // SAFETY AND PRIVACY

The rules she never breaks

These hold whatever the settings say, whoever asks, and in every version.
In-game lockoutwhile a game runs her hands never click, type or press keys. It can't be switched off, not even by voice or Blackwall mode.
Buyinga buy button, an order step or a payment always stops for your yes. She never types card numbers, IBANs or PINs.
Your words onlySECURITY, buying, caps, full access and the never-look list change by voice only when you clearly name them, never on a friend's or her own say-so.
Your data stays yours
On your PC and phoneher vault, your settings and keys (encrypted), her logs. Nothing is uploaded except the requests to the services you connect.
Never in the app or on GitHubfriends' names, your accounts, your address and school are kept out of the code, tests and releases. A check refuses to publish a release that contains any of them.
Screenshotsnever saved to disk; the never-look list hides apps and sites from her eyes
Updates
From our own releases onlythe updater reads only github.com/LittleTokyo1/capri-plus (installer, SHA256SUMS.txt, LICENSE.md) and checks the hash
Asks first, never during a gameshe tells you a new version is ready; it installs on your yes
One build for everyonethe build you run is the build your friends get, minus anything personal; features are switches with the same defaults
Every build runs a full smoke test of about 900 checks before it can ship5 / 5
